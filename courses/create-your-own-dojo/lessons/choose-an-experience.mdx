A learning experience gives someone room to explore, ask for hints, and build a skill with a tutor beside them.

An interview exercise can show how a candidate approaches a realistic task, pairs with an agent, and explains their choices. Start with a small example of everyday work, then describe what good collaboration looks like. Interview mode is a closed beta; this tutorial does not create a company interview or invite candidates.

The working authoring scaffold currently creates kata courses. We will use that supported path for the first draft. Interactive courses use a different authored format; choosing an interview purpose does not add a new manifest mode.

Write a two-sentence brief: who the experience is for, and what they will do. Keep it nearby for Kyoshi.
