Return to the same independent authoring draft. Improve the instruction or hint you noted during the trial, save it, and try the lesson again when you are ready.

Evaluation is a separate action. It can run configured models and judges, consumes model usage, and needs its own settings and lesson readiness. A successful trial is useful feedback; it is not a completed evaluation or a published course.

Keep the first draft small. You can add another lesson after one complete task feels clear to a learner.

Sharing and publication are later steps. Review the [publishing guide](https://dojo.foo/authoring/publish) before distributing anything. This tutorial does not publish your course or register it in the marketplace.

Ask your Sensei: “What should I review before someone else tries my course?” Tell them what you observed; the tutorial conversation cannot inspect your separate draft automatically.
