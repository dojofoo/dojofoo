Choose someone you want to help and one thing they should be able to do afterwards. Keep the first experience small enough to try in one sitting.

For example: “A teammate who knows basic JavaScript can combine default settings with a user's choices.” The outcome describes something you can observe, rather than a list of topics to cover.

Ask your Sensei: “Help me narrow my idea to one outcome.” Give them your audience and your first idea. They can help you shape the brief; they cannot create or edit the draft from this tutorial conversation.
