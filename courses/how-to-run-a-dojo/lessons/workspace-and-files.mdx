Draft starter course. This is a short orientation you can return to when something in Dojofoo feels unfamiliar.

Start at Courses to see the courses available in your local workspaces. Opening a course may ask you which workspace to use. Choose the folder where you want to keep your work; two workspaces can have different progress in the same course.

A normally installed course lives under `.dojos/<course-name>` in its workspace. Dojofoo also keeps local state, including interactive progress, in that workspace. Authoring drafts have their own source folder. Do not delete or move files while expecting an open session to follow them automatically.

The command `npx dojofoo status` reports the current workspace's course state. Use it from your learning folder when you need to confirm where you are.

Ask your Sensei: “Where are my files?” Include the workspace you selected if you are unsure which folder you opened.

The [quickstart](https://dojo.foo/docs/quickstart) explains the initial setup.
