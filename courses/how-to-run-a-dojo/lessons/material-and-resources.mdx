The course window explains the course and keeps its resources within reach. The sidebar lists the course’s lessons in a fixed order.

Inside a lesson, material can be split into slides. Use Previous and Next inside the material to review them. Changing a slide does not replace the sidebar’s lesson list or rename the course.

Questions are checkpoints. A correct answer unlocks what follows; an incorrect answer lets you try again. You can return to unlocked lessons through the sidebar without losing your answers.

Resources belong to the learning material. Open them when you need a reference, then return to the same lesson.
