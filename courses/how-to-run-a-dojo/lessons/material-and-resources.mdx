The sidebar shows where you are in the course. A course can offer a kata, interactive learning material, or supported video and slides. The material comes from that course; every course does not include every kind of content.

In a kata, use the file and test-result tabs to switch what you are looking at. The lesson's goal and resources explain what to work on. Keep the Sensei nearby when you need a hint.

When a slide deck is present, use Previous and Next below it; the page count shows your position. A video has its own playback controls. Resources appear with the relevant learning material rather than as invented files in the editor.

This introductory interactive course does not have a kata editor, video, slide deck, or test runner. Ask your Sensei to explain a control in your current course if it differs from another example.

See the [course formats](https://dojo.foo/authoring/manifest) for how material is authored.
