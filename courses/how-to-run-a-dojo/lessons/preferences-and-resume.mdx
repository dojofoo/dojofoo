Settings control how Dojo looks. Default is the standard theme; Auto makes the editor’s colors follow the theme. Light and dark appearance can be changed separately.

You can resize or hide the conversation. On a small screen, it opens in a separate panel. These controls change your view, not the course or its slides.

Return to the course from Courses to resume in its one workspace. Saved answers, unlocked lessons, and the agent’s conversation are separate from an unsent draft message. Only Send submits that message.

To create learning material, open “Create your own dojo”. The [authoring guide](https://dojo.foo/authoring/authoring-ui) explains the workflow.
