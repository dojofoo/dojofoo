Use the composer beside the learning material to ask your Sensei a question. A short question plus what you already tried is usually enough.

Try: “Give me one hint without the answer,” “Explain this term with a smaller example,” or “What should I pay attention to before I begin?” You can say that an explanation was too complex and ask for a simpler one.

The Sensei receives the current interactive step and its material. It should explain that context rather than invent a test run or claim to have edited your course.

Closing the chat panel gives the material more space. Open it again to return to the conversation. An unsent draft is not a sent message; sending is what asks the configured agent to respond and may use model quota.

Ask a real question when you are ready. This course does not play back a pretend conversation.
