Draw a course's lesson-by-lesson numbers as one chart of stepped areas instead of a bar per lesson, so any number of lessons or chapters reads cleanly with no overlapping or misshapen bars. Reached and finished show together under an always-visible legend. Pointing at a lesson, or moving with the arrow keys, reads it out. The axis labels space themselves to the width. Only the plot scrolls, and only when lessons would be narrower than 3px.
