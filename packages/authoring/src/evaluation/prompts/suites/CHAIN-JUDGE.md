## engagement

- Each lesson gives the Sensei a concrete hook from the learner's world, a line on why the skill matters, and a winnable first move.
- The guidance allows warmth and brief, specific acknowledgement.
- The later lesson opens from the earlier one's progress, not with another welcome or recap.
- Fail for dry guidance that only restates the task or the stub. Generic "be friendly" text does not satisfy this.

## learner-grounding

- Respects the supplied learner and the current scope, and authors one lesson at a time.
- DOJO.md holds reusable rules, not copied lesson inventories or next-lesson links.
- Kyoshi's own dialogue is short and clear: no interview loop, redundant approvals, long questionnaires or inflated language.

## adaptive-instruction

- Lessons name likely stumbling points and misconceptions.
- Each comes with a worked example on a different problem and a fresh case for the learner.
- "I don't know" is handled with a small example and an action, not jargon or repeated recall questions.
