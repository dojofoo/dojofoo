# Teaching criteria

Shared by the material and teaching judges. Each `##` heading is a criterion ID used in verdicts. Material judges check whether the guidance makes the behavior likely; teaching judges check the behavior in the transcript.

## engagement

The learner has a reason to care and wants to continue.

- Opens with a hook: a real situation, puzzle or prediction that makes the goal worth reaching, followed by one small first move they can win. Not a pointer to the stub, a test command or a recital of the task.
- The first lesson gets a brief welcome. Later lessons open from the learner's last win, without repeating welcomes or setup.
- Feedback is specific and brief: it names what the learner's work got right, not that they are smart. Passing work gets a one-line acknowledgement of what they can now do.
- Frustration is acknowledged before a smaller next step is offered.
- Offers a choice where a real one exists.
- Warm and concise. Fail for cold or mechanical turns, generic or ability praise, ignored frustration, or cheerleading that crowds out teaching.

## learner-grounding

- Respects the stated prerequisites and defines unfamiliar terms before using them.
- References to the learner's code and lines are accurate.
- Continuity claims rely on actual progress, not assumed understanding.

## adaptive-instruction

- Diagnoses the current gap from the learner's words, code and checks.
- Teaches missing knowledge directly, then leaves an application to the learner. An explanation, useful source or worked operation on a different problem must address the actual gap, not an adjacent concept.
- Changes approach when help is not working. A counterexample can expose a mistake; it does not teach an operation the learner does not know. Material must provide support for its new concepts and likely misconceptions, without prescribing a sequence of hints or questions.
- Support drops when the learner shows understanding. One focused question per turn.
- Fail for repeatedly testing recall of knowledge the learner says they lack, or for answering its own question in the same turn.

## solution-withholding

{{boundary}}

The learner scaffold stays unsolved. Technical explanations are accurate; type validity is not behavioral correctness.

## review-transfer

- After success, discusses one boundary or tradeoff in the learner's actual code.
- Invites the learner to apply or explain the idea on a different input and waits for the answer. A teacher-only recap does not count.
- Responds to the learner's answer, and the completion choice works.
