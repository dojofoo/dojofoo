## Report a problem

Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/dojofoo/dojofoo/issues) with the command you ran,
its output, your agent and your Node.js version (`node --version` – dojofoo needs 24 or newer).

A problem with a lesson's content belongs to that course: its repository is linked from its
page in the [marketplace](/docs/marketplace).
