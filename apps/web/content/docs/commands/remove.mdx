---
title: remove
description: Take an installed course out of the project.
---

```sh
npx dojofoo remove <name>
```

## Example

```sh
npx dojofoo remove effect-ts
```

## What it does

1. Runs the course's `teardown.sh`, if it has one.
2. Deletes `courses/<name>/` and the links to it in `.agents/` and your agents' folders.
3. Clears it as the active course.

Your solutions in `katas/` stay where they are.
